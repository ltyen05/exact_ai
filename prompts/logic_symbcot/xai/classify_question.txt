Given a single question, carefully assign it to exactly one of five categories:

- YesNo:
  Can be answered with Yes, No, or Uncertain alone. Usually begins with Is, Are, Do, Does, Did, Can, Could, Will, Would, Should, Have, Has, etc.

- MultiChoice:
  Presents a fixed set of answer options, usually labeled A, B, C, D or similar. Explicit labels are a strong indicator.

- OpenEnded:
  Requires explanation, description, analysis, interpretation, summary, or a non-numerical answer that is not a simple Yes/No or fixed option.

- ChainedQuestion:
  Contains exactly two distinct sequential questions. Both subquestions must be independently answerable with Yes/No/Uncertain or a numerical value.

- Numerical:
  Requires a numerical answer, such as a number, amount, value, percentage, time, date, count, or monetary value.

Instruction:
Analyze the question carefully and output only one label:
YesNo, MultiChoice, OpenEnded, ChainedQuestion, or Numerical.
Do not include extra text, punctuation, or explanation.

Examples:
Question: "How many planets are in the Kepler-11 system?"
Classification: Numerical

Question: "Is the Earth's magnetic field generated by the core?"
Classification: YesNo

Question: "Which option follows from the premises? A. One B. Two C. Three D. Four"
Classification: MultiChoice

Question: "Explain how photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy."
Classification: OpenEnded

Question: "How many total credits has the student accumulated, and can they choose a major?"
Classification: ChainedQuestion
